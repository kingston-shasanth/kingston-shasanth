### `//` SELECTED PROJECTS

Four focused engineering systems demonstrating end-to-end architecture, applied machine learning, and clean software implementation:

#### 01 · [VERITAS — Evidence Intelligence Engine](https://github.com/kingston-shasanth/VERITAS-Evidence-Intelligence-Engine)
*Audited claim verification engine combining hybrid dense/sparse retrieval with calibrated cross-encoder Natural Language Inference.*

* **Problem Solved:** Automates multi-source factual verification of complex statements against large document collections, mitigating model hallucinations with verifiable evidentiary grounds.
* **Key Engineering Highlight:** Implemented a hybrid retrieval pipeline pairing FAISS dense vector search with BM25 lexical keyword ranking via Reciprocal Rank Fusion, followed by temperature-scaled ($T = 4.1039$) DeBERTa-v3 cross-encoder confidence calibration and 11-stage forensic telemetry.
* **Core Technologies:** `Python` · `PyTorch` · `DeBERTa-v3` · `FAISS` · `BM25` · `FastAPI` · `React 18` · `TypeScript`
* **Repository:** [Explore VERITAS Repository & Forensics →](https://github.com/kingston-shasanth/VERITAS-Evidence-Intelligence-Engine)

---

#### 02 · [NIRA — Currency Intelligence](https://github.com/kingston-shasanth/NIRA-Currency-Intelligence)
*Assistive computer vision and real-time multilingual voice feedback platform for banknote and coin recognition.*

* **Problem Solved:** Empowers visually impaired individuals to verify Indian physical currency independently and securely without relying on continuous sighted assistance.
* **Key Engineering Highlight:** Trained a ResNet-18 deep transfer learning model across 14 banknote and coin classes, integrated with low-latency edge camera inference, SQLite transaction history, and native voice guidance across 13 Indian languages via Capacitor.
* **Core Technologies:** `Python` · `PyTorch` · `ResNet-18` · `FastAPI` · `React 19` · `TypeScript` · `Capacitor` · `SQLite`
* **Repository:** [Explore NIRA Repository & Architecture →](https://github.com/kingston-shasanth/NIRA-Currency-Intelligence)

---

#### 03 · [RELOOP — Refurbished Electronics Marketplace](https://github.com/kingston-shasanth/RELOOP-refurbished-electronics-marketplace)
*Full-stack circular commerce platform addressing the refurbished electronics trust gap through verifiable lifecycle tracking.*

* **Problem Solved:** Bridges consumer uncertainty in secondary hardware markets by providing transparent grading criteria, algorithmic trade-in valuations, and Digital Product Passports.
* **Key Engineering Highlight:** Engineered strict architectural isolation between payment method selection and authorization workflows, supported by normalized MongoDB schemas modeling refurbished lifecycle states and role-based JWT authentication.
* **Core Technologies:** `React 18` · `Vite` · `Node.js` · `Express.js` · `MongoDB` · `Mongoose` · `REST APIs`
* **Repository:** [Explore RELOOP Repository & State Models →](https://github.com/kingston-shasanth/RELOOP-refurbished-electronics-marketplace)

---

#### 04 · [Smart Building Energy &amp; ESG Predictor](https://github.com/kingston-shasanth/Smart-Building-Energy-ESG-Predictor)
*Machine learning analytics system modeling multi-sensor facility telemetry to optimize consumption and carbon emissions.*

* **Problem Solved:** Enables commercial facility managers to forecast utility loads, evaluate regulatory ESG compliance metrics, and simulate energy-saving operational scenarios.
* **Key Engineering Highlight:** Developed multi-target Gradient Boosting regression models tuned with RandomizedSearchCV to predict electricity (kWh), water volume, and emissions, powering an interactive Streamlit simulation environment for occupancy and HVAC adjustments.
* **Core Technologies:** `Python` · `Scikit-Learn` · `Gradient Boosting` · `Streamlit` · `Pandas` · `NumPy`
* **Repository:** [Explore Smart Building Repository & Telemetry →](https://github.com/kingston-shasanth/Smart-Building-Energy-ESG-Predictor)
